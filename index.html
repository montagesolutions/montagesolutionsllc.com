About Us

Built for Government. Driven by Results.

Montage Solutions LLC is a New Mexico-based government contracting firm specializing in facilities support services, project management, and operational coordination. We partner with federal agencies and local governments to deliver dependable, professionally managed services on time and on budget.

Founded by Darie Monahan, Montage Solutions is structured to move quickly, communicate clearly, and execute with precision. Every contract receives direct owner involvement. No account managers, no handoffs, no gaps in accountability.
